Past and Present

Autofiction,

Metaprose,

Existential prose.

“

Sometimes you just need to go to a cemetery on a dark evening.

I have nothing to add

Debating with myself. With myself from the past. Probably not. A relay baton, something like that. Yes, the one I passed to my future self in a cozy home.

My past self is suffering right now. My hands are stained with ink. Purity of consciousness?

I don't know with which exact self I'm communicating. Personality as such doesn't exist, there's only a desire to seem to be the one you are. For yourself and others. And it is constant.

Here they are, our first sentences. It was easy for me. From page to page.

1: I wasn't scared. I wasn't in a hurry home. A rain cloud was crossing with the wild mold of the autumn sunset.

Fool, I wrote in the notebook skipping every second page. That's why it ended so quickly.

3: The wind crumbled and scattered the lonely grass. I was walking to the cemetery. Though, in my soul I stood... Maybe they were waiting for me there?

The text was becoming more and more shaky. In my slow walk one could feel a crookedness. Nevertheless, I had enough time for beautiful images.

5: I saw nothing beyond my sick notebook. In some sense, everything that I described
7: or where I was gave empty. It's about nothing at all. But I kept the mute path to the first stop.

Stop, don't rush. Better be slower. It's hard for me to make out your words. I bet it will only get worse further.

9: I spent my thoughts and ink as inefficiently as possible. I already saw nothing of what I write.

Yes, I think I can forgive myself. I wanted this dialogue to be full-fledged. So that I could hear my past self and answer him. Just continue.

11: My only hope is only that, having seen the light, I'll decipher what I wrote about. And if it can be read...

You're right, I can read it. But what will be next? Will every word remain shared?

13: so, I succeeded. But I got scared for my life. And Δ back. Dark Δ sky.

Here it starts. Dear friend, I don't understand you. But I feel the meaning. Because of my physiology? Yes, I wanted to say that I went back and the dark began to eat the sky.

15: Now I'm really scared. Help. Where is my turn home?

Maybe I did start trembling. But I tried to draw out the words in the notebook precisely. Yeah, I love you too.

17: So who should I ask? My running-out ink? And if I'm not Δ?

Actually, don't. You have no one to ask. You're lonely. I'm with you, but I have to keep you lonely, otherwise our spiritual superposition will turn into madness.

19: After all, I know the serenity of this moment. But I'm free to fix it. Not in my consciousness, but in this thin notebook.

Did you even know that you could communicate with me? Is this process considered communication if you don't hear me? Yet my phrases still reach you...

21: I Δ and have almost used it up.

The key to understanding is memory. But how then are people to understand each other if they can't share it. And so you will not understand this sentence. I will.

23: Funny. I don't even know if there's a need to write further.

If I have nothing to say, then I just stay silent?

25: Because of the darkness I don't know if there's any ink left.

Honestly, it would be better if it ran out. You're going too far. Our connection is getting lost.

27: Δ when will these words gain meaning?

29: When I imagined them, experienced them, wrote them or read them?

Maybe when I answered?

31: Will I be able to do at least something? These reflections can't last forever. The notebook is coming to an end.

For some reason it seemed to me that after reading my scribbles and writing them down here I would find something special. I would be able to reflect, come to something. But there's no concreteness in essence. It's empty existentialism, when the checkered sheets live their own life. You communicate, try to tell them something. But in reality — its just an euthanasia of thought.

33: I'm no longer scared. Home is so close. A lonely window illuminating the street noisy from Δ.

How hard it is to make out... I'm lying. Specifically in this moment I could make it out perfectly, but I preferred to hide this word.
So, it's not a dialogue. Here I hold the dominant position, since I'm free to change words.
What's the meaning of all this then?

35: Why write about the impermanent if the moment is eternal?

Yes, it is such while I'm able to remember it through blind typing of letters. But it changes every time. It's eternal in its diversity. I've gone far. As far as I remember, there will be something important here. I must make it out...

37: It is rendered in my politeness to existence and shown in the form of illegible inscriptions on the go.
39: If I get home and can't understand what I wrote, will this put an end to the story?

If I understood nothing, probably this text would become much heavier.

41: Or will it give beginning to the next chapter? Moonlight allows me to still be sure of my existence.

You're mistaken. Not just in existence, but in the duality of our communication. I no longer know: should I hate or adore you. Next time I'll need to warn you about the dialogue in advance. Will there even be a next time? Do I need this new experience?

43: Silence and fear are interchangeable concepts.

When I'm silent, are you scared or not?

45: Words less and less find the need to tell about themselves. This means it's time to finish.

Are you scared that you'll finish? That you'll plunge into eternal silence?
Silence where I won't communicate with you anymore? Honestly, you're the best interlocutor.

47: It's time to open the door and enter my arm home. To find out what will be next...

Yes, that's all. Are you satisfied with what you learned? Did I become your friend? Shall we repeat?
